Some of Over Adventures

Family Splash & Scenic Drift

Enjoy a relaxing and fun day on the water! This trip is perfect for families, first-time rafters, and anyone looking for a gentle river adventure. You will float through breathtaking scenery, experience mild and splashing rapids, and have plenty of opportunities to take a swim in calm pools. Our certified guides will ensure a safe, fun, and memorable journey for all ages.

The Coroico Canyon Express

Get ready for an adrenaline-pumping experience as you navigate through continuous, exciting Class III rapids! This full-day adventure takes you deep into the heart of the lush canyon, surrounded by stunning mountain views and tropical waterfalls. Mid-way through the journey, we stop at a riverside beach for a freshly prepared buffet lunch before tackling the second half of the river’s action-packed waves.

Devil's Gorge Challenge

Conquer the wildest section of the river! Designed exclusively for confident swimmers and thrill-seekers looking for the ultimate rush, "Devil's Gorge" delivers fast, technical, and massive whitewater rapids. Working as a team under the direction of our top expedition guides, you will power through narrow chutes, drops, and intense wave trains. Safety gear, high-performance rafts, and a pre-trip technical briefing are provided.
