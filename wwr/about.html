History

AquaRush Rafting started with just two rafts and a vision to share the excitement of white water sports with everyone. Over the past decade, we have grown into a premier rafting company, guided thousands of adventurers down thrilling rapids, and maintained an unblemished safety record. Our journey continues as we bring high-energy water experiences to beginners and seasoned rafters alike
